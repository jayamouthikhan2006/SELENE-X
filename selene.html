01.SENSOR LAYER

Always watching. Ultra-low power.

Reads and processes data from multiple sensors in real time
using the Snapdragon Sensing Hub.

  • Accelerometermotion, impacts, falls, gait
  • Gyroscopeorientation change, tumbles, rotation
  • Magnetometer / Compassheading, orientation confirmation
  • GNSSlocation, speed, route deviation (dual-frequency L1+L5)
  • Ambient lightdarkness, in-pocket vs in-hand
  • Proximityphone leaving a pocket, face-down detection
  • Microphonecrash sounds, screams, alarms (event detection only)
  • Camerainjuries, road signs, documents, thief capture
